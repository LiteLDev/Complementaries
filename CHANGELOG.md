## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-29

First release: the blocks, items, mechanics, screens and vanilla tweaks of Supplementaries 4.0.2,
on LeviLamina 26.51.7 (Minecraft Bedrock 1.26.51), for the dedicated server and the client.

### Added

- Jars that hold fluids, cookies or small mobs, the soft fluids (water, lava, honey, milk, potions,
  soups, experience, lumisene, ...) and the faucet.
- Storage: sack, present and trapped present, quiver, lunch basket.
- Locks: key, lock block, safe, iron and gold gates, gold and netherite doors and trapdoors.
- Signs: sign posts for every wood, way signs near villages, notice board, doormat.
- Blackboard with a drawing screen, chalk and dye drawing, glow and wax.
- Instruments: globe and sepia globe, wind vane, clock block, hourglass, altimeter.
- Mechanisms: spring launcher, bellows, turn table, cog block, crank (which also turns the pulley).
- Redstone: relayer, speaker block, crystal display, redstone illuminator, minecart with dispenser.
- Lighting: sconces, candle holders, stone lamps, wall lanterns.
- Building blocks: ash, ash bricks, stone and blackstone tiles, lapis bricks, checker blocks, daub and
  timber frames, raked gravel, fine wood, soap, flint, feather and sugar blocks, with their stairs,
  slabs and walls.
- Rope, rope arrow, pulley, flax crop, wild flax, flax block.
- Displays: pedestal, item shelf, hat stand, statue.
- Tableware: goblet, urn, planter, flower box, book piles, pancake, candy, jar boat.
- Flags with banner patterns, awnings, buntings, the Supplementaries banner patterns.
- Cannon, cannonball, bombs, red merchant.
- Tools: slingshot, bubble blower, soap, wrench, flute, confetti popper, antique ink, slice map.
- Traps: bamboo spikes, tipped spikes, cage, fodder.
- Vanilla tweaks: placeable sticks, rods and gunpowder; throwable bricks and slimeballs; hanging
  flower pots; clock and compass info; experience bottling; bell chains; cauldron dyeing; zombie
  horses.
- `/supplementaries` (alias `/complementaries`) with `reload`, `selftest` and the feature
  subcommands listed in the README.
- One configuration file per feature group under `config/`, reloaded by `/supplementaries reload`.
- English and Simplified Chinese translations.
